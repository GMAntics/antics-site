Christmas dares

  1. It’s ten seconds to midnight on New Year’s Eve. Count the room down from ten, then kiss whoever’s nearest on the cheek.
  2. Be the Christmas tree. The room has thirty seconds to decorate you with whatever’s to hand.
  3. Pretend the person on your left just gave you the worst present ever. Open it and thank them like you mean it.
  4. Put every player here on Santa’s naughty or nice list, and say why.
  5. Text the last person you kissed: “Merry Christmas x”.
  6. Hold your hand over the person on your left and the next person along like mistletoe. They kiss on the cheek or both do a forfeit.
  7. Act out a Christmas film with no words. The room has thirty seconds to guess it.
  8. Be a shopping centre Santa. The person on your left sits on your knee and tells you what they want for Christmas.
  9. Let the person on your left pick a New Year’s resolution for you. Break it before your next turn and you do a forfeit.
  10. Lead the room in a Christmas song. Anyone who hasn’t joined in by the chorus does a forfeit.
  11. Wake the person on your left up like an excited kid on Christmas morning.
  12. Run into the person on your left’s arms like the end of a Christmas film.

How to play Christmas Truth or Dare

  1. Sit in a rough circle, because some cards name the person on your left or the next person along.
  2. On your turn, tap the button and read the card out. A question is a truth and an instruction is a dare, and the shuffle decides which you get.
  3. Answer the truth honestly, or do the dare there and then. Everyone gets one pass a game.
  4. Then the person on your left goes next.

Prefer to choose? Say truth or dare first, then tap until that kind of card comes up. Three of the dares can end in a forfeit, so agree what one is before you start: there are ideas on the forfeit ideas page. The classic rules and longer lists are in the Truth or Dare guide.

Christmas Truth or Dare: things people ask

Are these Christmas truths and dares clean?

Not all of them. A few are about exes, kissing and the work Christmas party, so read the list first if the room is mixed. For a work do, the clean deck on the Truth or Dare generator has no innuendo and no swearing.

When are the Christmas cards in the Antics app?

From 1 December to 1 January, free for everyone. Switch Christmas on in the Truth or Dare lobby before you start, and they are dealt in among the usual cards. Never Have I Ever, Most Likely To, Would You Rather and Antics mode have Christmas cards too.

How many people do you need?

Three or more. Four to eight is best, so turns come round quickly and nobody waits long for a go.
