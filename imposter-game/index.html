How to play Imposter on one phone

  1. Set how many are playing and pick a category. Leave the hint on if the imposter needs a lifeline.
  2. Pass the phone round. Each player taps to see their role, then passes it on. Everyone gets the same secret word, except the imposter.
  3. Go round the circle, one clue each, without saying the word. The imposter bluffs along.
  4. On three, everyone points at their suspect. Then reveal the imposter. If they were caught, they get one guess at the word, and a right guess still wins.

Imposter online: questions

Can you play Imposter on one phone?

Yes, that is what this page is for. Pass it round, everyone taps to see their role, then put the phone down and start the clues.

How many players do you need?

Three or more, up to twelve. Four to eight is the sweet spot.

What does the imposter’s hint do?

It gives the imposter one word linked to the secret, like Cheese for Pizza. True, but it only gets them so far. Switch it off for a harder round.
