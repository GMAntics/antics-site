What each game plays like

Every game here is pass the phone: one person reads the card out, the room plays it, and the phone moves on. A round deals ten of the Antics app’s free cards. Here are real cards from each game.

Never Have I Ever

The card names something. Anyone who has done it owns up, and the stories come out.

Never have I ever… sent a message to the group chat that was meant for one person.

Truth or Dare

Pick truth and answer it honestly, or pick dare and do it.

Truth: Who in this room survives longest in a horror film, and who goes first?

Dare: Do your best impression of the player on your left.

Most Likely To

Read it out, count to three, and everyone points at who fits it best.

Most likely to… accidentally join a cult.

Would You Rather

Two options. Everyone picks at once, then defends their choice.

Would you rather… lick a bus pole, or eat a crisp off the club floor?

What Are The Odds

Dare a mate. You both count down from three and shout a number from 1 to 10. If the numbers match, it happens.

What are the odds… you crawl a full lap of the room, commentating like a nature documentary.
