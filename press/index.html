Antics press kit

Antics is a party game app that puts nine pass-the-phone party games on one phone, on iPhone and Android, in eleven language versions. There are no ads and no accounts: download it, pass the phone round and play.

Fast facts

Antics at a glance
NameAntics. On the App Store as “Antics: Party Games & Dares”.
What it isA party game app: one phone runs the whole night and gets passed round the room, so nobody has to host.
GamesNine, including Antics mode, Imposter, Never Have I Ever, Truth or Dare, Most Likely To, Would You Rather, What Are The Odds and The Four Kings.
CardsThousands, hand-picked by the Antics team. A phone won’t repeat a card until it has shown the lot.
PlatformsiPhone and Android.
PriceFree to download. Imposter and The Four Kings are free to play (two Imposter decks unlock free when you follow Antics on Instagram or TikTok), and most other games come with free cards. Antics Premium unlocks the full decks; prices are shown in the app.
LanguagesEleven versions: English, Spanish (Spain and Latin America), Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil), German, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Czech.
AdsNone.
AccountsNone. No sign-up, no login.
DrinkingOptional. Nothing in the app depends on drinking, so everyone plays the same game.
AgeRated 18+ on the App Store.
Made byThe Antics team, in the UK. Published by Antics App Ltd. Not connected to any other app or site called Antics.
Press contactsupport@anticsapp.com

Party data and charts

We publish anonymous figures from the parties played on Antics: percentages and ratios only, never names, answers or anything anyone typed. The first release covers 26 August to 4 October 2026. Four things it found:

  • 9pm is the busiest hour to start a party, UK time.
  • A Saturday night gets 1.8 times as many parties as a Monday to Thursday night.
  • Three in four parties play more than one game.
  • 64% of Truth or Dare games open with a truth.

The method, every chart and a CSV of every figure are on the party data page. The numbers are free to use under CC BY 4.0, and the charts below come with the source set into the image.
