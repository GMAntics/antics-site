How do you play Never Have I Ever?

Someone reads out a card that finishes the line “Never have I ever…”. Anyone who has done it owns up, by putting a finger down or doing the forfeit you agreed at the start. Then the next person reads.

Can you play Never Have I Ever as a drinking game?

Yes. Plenty of groups play it on a night out or at pre-drinks. The Antics app never tells anyone to drink and nothing in it depends on drinking, so the whole room plays the same game, drinking or not.
