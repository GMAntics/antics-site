The Four Kings: a rule for every card

52 cards, 13 rules, one circle. Draw a card, do what it says, and hope you are not the one holding the fourth King.

How to play

  1. Sit in a circle.
  2. Open The Four Kings in Antics, which deals all 52 cards for you. Or spread a real pack face down in the middle and keep this page open as the rule sheet.
  3. Take turns drawing one card.
  4. Every card has a rule. Do what it says.
  5. Keep going until the fourth King comes out.

The rule for every card

AThe Wave
Everyone holds a squat together. You can stand whenever you like. Everyone else can only stand when the person on their right stands.
2You
Point at a player. They take a forfeit.
3Me
Unlucky, that one’s on you. Take a forfeit.
4Ladies
Ladies, that’s you, every girl takes a forfeit.
5Thumbmaster
You’re the Thumbmaster until the next 5. Sneak your thumb onto the table at any moment, last player to copy you takes a forfeit.
6Gents
Gents, that’s you, every guy takes a forfeit.
7Heaven
Reach for the sky! Last player to raise a hand takes a forfeit.
8Mate
Choose a mate. Until the next 8 is drawn, whenever one of you gets a forfeit, you both do it.
9Rhyme
Say a word. Go around the circle rhyming with it. First to hesitate or repeat takes a forfeit.
10Categories
Pick a category: cereals, footballers, things in a pencil case. Go around naming them. First to blank takes a forfeit.
JRule Maker
Invent a rule (no first names, no saying “yes”…). It stands until the next Jack. Rule-breakers take a forfeit.
QQuestion Master
You’re the Question Master until the next Queen. Anyone who answers a question you ask takes a forfeit.
KThe Fourth King
Add one move to the group dance routine. Whoever draws the FOURTH King performs the whole routine, start to finish.

The cards that stick

Four cards give someone a job that lasts until the same card is drawn again: the Thumbmaster (5), the Mate (8), the Rule Maker (Jack) and the Question Master (Queen). These are where the grudges start.

The fourth King

Each King adds one move to a group dance routine. Nobody minds the first three. Whoever draws the fourth performs the lot, start to finish, with the whole room watching.

Need forfeits?

Questions people ask

Do we need a pack of cards?

No. The app deals all 52. A real pack works too.

Is The Four Kings free?

Yes. The whole game is free in Antics.

Is it a drinking game?

Not this version. Every rule ends in a forfeit.

What happens when a rule card comes up twice?

The job passes on. A new Thumbmaster, Mate, Rule Maker or Question Master takes over from the old one.
