How to play Imposter online with friends

You need three to twelve players, a phone each and an internet connection. A laptop works too.

  1. Start a room. One of you opens anticsapp.com/j/?host=1, types a name, picks the options and taps Create the room.
  2. Share the code. The room gets a short code and a link. Tap Share the join link and send it to the group chat, or just read the code out.
  3. Everyone joins. Your friends open the link, or go to anticsapp.com/j and type the code. They add a name and tap Join the room. No download, no sign-up.
  4. Deal the round. Once three or more are in, whoever started the room taps Deal the round.
  5. Look at your card. Everyone taps to look and keeps their screen to themselves. Most of you see the secret word. The imposter sees “You are the imposter”, plus a one-word hint if hints are on.
  6. Give clues. Your screen says who goes first. Go round, one clue each, without saying the word. The imposter bluffs along.
  7. Vote, then reveal. On three, everyone names their suspect. Caught them? The imposter gets one guess at the word before anyone taps reveal, and a right guess still wins. Then anyone in the round taps Reveal the imposter, and every phone shows who it was and the word.

After the reveal, whoever is dealing taps Deal the next round. New to the game? How to play Imposter has the full rules.

What you can switch on

Each one is a tick on the start screen. Tick the ones you want before you create the room.

  • Imposter gets a hint. On unless you untick it. The imposter gets one word linked to the secret, like Cheese for Pizza.
  • Hard hints. In English only. A less obvious hint for a tougher round. Pizza's hard hint is Friday.
  • Two imposters. From six players. Each imposter knows there are two of them, but not who the other one is.
  • Mischief mode. Now and then, every phone says you're the imposter and nobody has the word. Watch the room try to bluff its way out.

Good to know

  • 3 to 12 players. You need three to deal, and six for two imposters.
  • Free decks. A room started on this site deals the four free decks: Locations, Everyday Objects, Famous People, and Food and Drinks. No word comes round twice in a room until its deck runs out.
  • Language. Each phone shows the room in its own browser's language. The words come in the language of the browser that started the room, or in English if Antics isn't in that language.
  • Late arrivals. Someone who joins mid-round sits that one out and gets dealt into the next.
  • Names. Up to 16 characters. The name you type is deleted when the room closes, and never goes into our analytics.
  • Rooms close on their own. A room closes a few hours after its last round, and never stays open longer than twelve hours. Start a fresh one any time.
  • Leaving. Before the first deal, whoever started the room can remove a player. If the person who started the room leaves, the next player on the room's list takes over dealing.

Who it suits

  • A group on a video call. Keep the call open and play the room on your phones. Clues and the vote happen on the call. The cards stay on your own screens.
  • The pub or halls. Everyone has their phone out anyway. Nothing gets passed across a sticky table, and your card never leaves your hand.
  • A big, spread-out room. Twelve of you across the sofa, the floor and the kitchen door. Nobody waits for a phone to reach them.

Different phones or passing a phone round?

Both are free in the browser. In a room, every card lands on its own phone. You can play apart, nobody waits their turn to look, and nobody sees a screen they shouldn't. Every phone needs a connection for the whole game.

Passing a phone round suits a table where you're all together and the other phones can stay in pockets. Our Imposter game page deals that way: pick a category, pass it round, and everyone taps to see their role.

Imposter online: FAQ

Can you play Imposter online with friends?

Yes. Antics runs free online multiplayer Imposter rooms. Start one at anticsapp.com/j/?host=1, share the code, and everyone joins in their own browser. Every phone shows its own card, so it works round a table or on a video call.

Does everyone need the Antics app?

No. A room runs in the browser, on a phone or a laptop, with no download and no sign-up.

How many players can join an Imposter room?

Three to twelve. You need three to deal, and six or more for two imposters.

Can you play Imposter on a video call?

Yes. Keep the call going and open the room on your phones. Give clues and vote on the call, and keep your card to yourself.

Is it free?

Yes. A room started on this site is free, with no ads. It deals the four free decks, and the Antics app has more.

How long does a room last?

It closes on its own a few hours after the last round, and the names typed into it are deleted. Start a new room any time.
