What you get with Antics

1. The feed, and eight more games

If what you like about Picolo is one feed of dares, votes and rules, Antics has that: it’s called Antics mode and it’s the flagship. Rules stay in force for a few cards and count down while you play, and cards call people out by name. The difference is that it’s one game on the shelf, not the whole app, so when the room wants something else you switch to Imposter, Never Have I Ever or The Four Kings and keep going.

Picolo’s App Store description pitches it as a change from “the same old games”, and it names Truth or Dare and Never Have I Ever among them. We took the other view: those games are classics because they work. They just need better cards than the lists everyone has already read.

2. Still being written

Antics was last updated in October 2026, and new cards land every few weeks: the ones groups skip get retired, and fresh ones take their place. For the record, Picolo’s iPhone app is on version 2.3.1 from March 2023, per its App Store version history, and its Android app was last updated in May 2026, per Google Play.

3. Pay the way you like

Antics Premium unlocks the full decks. On iPhone it’s weekly, with the first week free for new subscribers, or monthly, or a one-time Forever purchase with nothing to renew. Android’s options differ slightly, and the app shows them with prices before you pay. Picolo’s App Store listing describes its subscription as one week with a three-day trial, one month or one year.

Worth knowing, and this is how every App Store free trial works, ours included: a trial becomes a paid subscription automatically unless you cancel before it ends, and cancelling doesn’t refund a charge that has already gone through. You can check anything you’re subscribed to under Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.
