Antics in brief

What is Antics?

A party game app for iPhone and Android. Nine pass-the-phone games on one phone: Antics mode, The Four Kings, Never Have I Ever, Truth or Dare, Most Likely To, Would You Rather, What Are The Odds, Imposter and Spicy. Thousands of cards, and the app remembers which ones your phone has shown so you get new ones before any come round again. Nothing in it depends on drinking, so drinkers and non-drinkers play the same game.

Is Antics free?

Free to download. Imposter and The Four Kings are free (two Imposter decks unlock free when you follow Antics on Instagram or TikTok), and every other game except Spicy comes with free cards. Premium unlocks the full decks and Spicy: a weekly subscription (the first week is free for new subscribers on iPhone), a monthly subscription, or a one-off Forever purchase. Prices are shown in the app. One of you pays. The whole room plays.

Can everyone play Imposter on their own phone?

Yes. The host starts a room at anticsapp.com/j/?host=1, friends join with the room code, and nobody needs the app.

Who is it for?

Adult groups. Rated 18+ on the App Store. No ads, no accounts, no sign-up. Plays in eleven languages: English, Spanish (Latin America and Spain), Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), German, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Czech.

Who makes it?

Guy Matthews, a solo founder in London, through Antics App Ltd. Antics is the app at anticsapp.com, not connected to playantics.com or antics.gg, which are separate browser game sites. More about Antics. Official store pages: Antics on the App Store and Antics on Google Play.
