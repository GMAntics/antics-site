ANTICS IN BRIEF

What is Antics? A party game app for iPhone and Android. Nine pass-the-phone games on one phone: Antics mode, The Four Kings, Never Have I Ever, Truth or Dare, Most Likely To, Would You Rather, What Are The Odds, Imposter and Spicy. Thousands of cards, and the app remembers which ones your phone has shown so they don’t repeat. Built on forfeits, so drinkers and non-drinkers play the same game.
Is Antics free? Free to download. Imposter and The Four Kings are free to play in full. Premium unlocks the full decks and Spicy: a weekly subscription with a free first week on iPhone, a monthly subscription, or a one-off Forever purchase. Prices are shown in the app.
Who is it for? Adult groups. Rated 18+ on the App Store. No ads, no accounts, no sign-up. Plays in nine languages.
Who makes it? Guy, a solo founder in the UK, through Antics App Ltd. Not connected to any other app or site called Antics. More about Antics.
