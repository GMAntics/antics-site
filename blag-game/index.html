Blag: one truth, a table full of lies

The bluffing game for everyone's own phone. Write a lie good enough to pass for the truth, sniff out the real answer, and find out who in your group lies best.

How to play

  1. One person opens Blag in the Antics app and starts a room. The app shows a short code and a link to share.
  2. Everyone else joins on their own phone. They can open the link in a browser and play free, with nothing to download and no account, or join from the Antics app if they have it.
  3. A question goes up. One player answers it truthfully; everyone else writes a fake that sounds just like them. Then all the answers go on every phone, shuffled, and everybody votes for the one they think is real.

Fool someone with your lie and you score. Spot the truth and you score. The truth-teller scores for every person who misses it, so they want an answer that sounds made up. The last round counts double, and the phones tell you who fell for whom.

Two ways to play

About us

Three to ten players. Each round puts one of you in the spotlight: they tell the truth, the rest of the room writes the lie they think that person would tell. It is a game about how well you know each other, and about how well you can do an impression of your mates in writing.

Who said that?

Four to eight players. Everyone answers the same question honestly, and then the room guesses who wrote what. The trick is to tell the truth in somebody else's voice.

Stuck for a lie?

Tap Lie for me and the House writes one for you. Act normal. The House sometimes fools the whole table, which is its own kind of embarrassing.

Played on phones, not the TV

Blag needs no screen in the corner and no console. Every player reads, writes and votes on the phone in their hand, so it works in a bedroom, a kitchen, a pub or a long car journey, wherever you have a signal. Only the host needs the app.

Answers are typed, so the quiet one in the group gets the same shot as the loudest, and nobody can tell from your face which answer is yours. That is the whole point.

How to lie well

  • Steal their voice. If they always say "honestly" or never use capitals, neither do you.
  • Be boring on purpose. A dull, specific answer passes for the truth far more often than a funny one.
  • Leave the truth alone. If you are the one telling it, do not polish it. Real answers are often the strangest thing on the list.
  • Watch the votes, not the answers. Whoever looks pleased with themselves at the reveal wrote the lie that worked.

Questions people ask

Does everyone need the app?

No. Only the host needs Antics. Everyone else joins free in their phone's browser with the room code or link.

How many people can play?

About us takes three to ten players. Who said that? takes four to eight.

Is Blag free?

Yes. Blag comes with free cards in the Antics app, and joining a room costs nothing. When a Premium player hosts, every card is unlocked for the whole room.

Is it a drinking game?

No. You win on points, by lying well and spotting the truth.
