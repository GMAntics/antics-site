How to play The Four Kings online

  1. Sit in a circle with one phone. Put it in the middle or pass it round.
  2. On your turn, tap the card to draw. Read out its name and its rule.
  3. Do what it says. When a card calls for a forfeit, it suggests one, or the room can invent its own.
  4. Four cards give someone a job that lasts until the same card comes up again: the Thumbmaster (5), the Mate (8), the Rule Maker (Jack) and the Question Master (Queen).
  5. Each King adds a move to the group dance routine, and the counter shows how many are out. Hope you are not the one who draws the fourth.
  6. The game runs to the last of the 52 cards. Then shuffle and play again.

It is the same circle card game, which plenty of people call Kings Cup or Ring of Fire. The Four Kings is the Antics version, and every card is a forfeit or a challenge.

Do we need a pack of cards?

No. The page deals all 52 cards, keeps count of the Kings and suggests a forfeit whenever a card calls for one.

What happens on the fourth King?

Every King card reads: “Add one move to the group dance routine. Whoever draws the FOURTH King performs the whole routine, start to finish.” The counter under the card shows how many Kings are out, and the deck carries on to the last card.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, yes. The whole deck is already on your phone, so a bad signal will not stop the game.
