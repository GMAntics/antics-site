1. Antics (our pick)

Nine pass-the-phone party games on one phone: Antics mode, Never Have I Ever, Truth or Dare, Most Likely To, Would You Rather, What Are The Odds, Imposter, The Four Kings and Spicy. There are thousands of cards, and your phone remembers what it’s shown, so nothing repeats until you’ve seen the lot.

We’d put it first for a group where some people are drinking and some aren’t. Every game runs on forfeits, and the app never tells anyone to drink, so everyone plays the same game.

Antics at a glance
Best forMixed groups and nights where you want to switch games without switching apps.
How you playOne phone, passed round. Add names and some cards call people out.
PayingFree to download. Imposter and The Four Kings are free to play, and every other game except Spicy comes with free cards. Antics Premium, a subscription or a one-off Forever purchase, unlocks the full decks and Spicy.
PlatformsiPhone and Android. No ads, no accounts.
LanguagesEnglish, Spanish (Spain and Latin America), Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil), German, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Czech: eleven versions in all. Spicy is in English.
Age rating18+ on the App Store.
Worth knowingIt’s new (out since 2026) and much smaller than the apps below, with far fewer ratings. It isn’t on iPad.

Try before you download: a free round in your browser, or Imposter online. Every game is on the party games page.

3. Heads Up!

4. Exposed

Party game apps: FAQ

What’s the best party game app?

It depends on the group. For charades, Heads Up! is the big name. For a group where not everyone is drinking, we’d pick Antics, but we make it, so we would. The table on this page shows how the rest compare.

Is there a party game app that isn’t a drinking game?

Yes. Antics runs every game on forfeits and never tells anyone to drink. Splash says on its listing that it isn’t meant as a drinking game, and Heads Up!, Psych! and Wavelength are guessing and bluffing games.

Which party game apps work on one phone?

Antics, Heads Up!, Picolo, Splash and Boomit are played on one phone. Exposed, Psych!, Wavelength and Evil Apples have everyone playing on their own phone.

Are party game apps free?

Most are free to download, with paid extras. Heads Up! costs £1.99 on iPhone and is free to download on Android. Antics is free to download: Imposter and The Four Kings are free to play, the other games except Spicy come with free cards, and Antics Premium unlocks the rest.

Why isn’t Jackbox on this list?

Jackbox Party Packs are great, but they aren’t a phone app. One person runs the game on a screen the whole room can see, and everyone else joins at jackbox.tv and uses their phone as a controller. This list is apps that run on the phone itself.
