1. Antics (our pick)

Antics is the one we’d hand round at any party: nine pass-the-phone games, thousands of hand-picked cards, no ads and nothing to set up. Antics mode, Never Have I Ever, Truth or Dare, Most Likely To, Would You Rather, What Are The Odds, Imposter, The Four Kings and Spicy all live on one phone, so the night never stalls while someone hunts for another app.

Every card is hand-picked by the Antics team. Cards that don’t land get retired, fresh ones arrive every few weeks, and your phone won’t repeat a card until you’ve seen the lot. Nothing in it depends on drinking, so drinkers and non-drinkers play the same game.

3. Evil Apples

A fill-in-the-blank card game. Everyone picks an answer card to finish a question card, and a judge picks the funniest. Made by Super Massive Ventures.

Evil Apples at a glance
How you playOnline, everyone on their own phone, with friends or in quick games with strangers.

4. Exposed

10. Wavelength

The official app of the Wavelength board game. One player gives a clue, and the team turns a dial to guess where it sits between two opposites, like hot and cold. Made by Palm Court and CMYK Games.

Wavelength at a glance
How you playEveryone on their own phone, 2 to 10 players, as one team. It needs an internet connection, even in the same room (its FAQ).
PayingFree with no ads, its listing says. Optional content packs are in-app purchases.
PlatformsiPhone, iPad and Android.
Age rating9+ on the App Store.

Party game apps: FAQ

What’s the best party game app?

We’d say Antics, but we make it, so we would. It puts nine games on one phone, from Never Have I Ever to Imposter, with thousands of hand-picked cards, no ads and no accounts, so one app covers mates, uni, a hen do or a dinner party. Nothing in it depends on drinking, so drinkers and non-drinkers play the same game.

Is there a party game app that isn’t a drinking game?

Yes. Antics never tells anyone to drink and nothing in it depends on drinking, so the whole room plays the same game, whoever is drinking.

Can you play a party game app on one phone?

Yes. Every game in Antics is played on one phone passed round, with no accounts. Some other apps, such as Exposed, Psych! and Wavelength, need everyone to have the app on their own phone.

Are party game apps free?

Antics is free to download, with no ads: Imposter and The Four Kings are free to play, and the other games except Spicy come with free cards. New subscribers on iPhone can try Antics Premium free for a week; after that it becomes a weekly subscription unless you cancel. Most other apps here are free to download with paid extras; Heads Up! costs £1.99 on iPhone.

Why isn’t Jackbox on this list?

Jackbox Party Packs aren’t a phone app. One person runs the game on a screen the whole room can see, and everyone else joins at jackbox.tv and uses their phone as a controller. This list is apps that run on the phone itself.
