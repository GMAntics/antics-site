How party games work without drinking

A lot of classic party games end a round with a drink. Antics ends it with a forfeit instead: a short, silly penalty the app deals out, like giving tomorrow’s weather forecast in your best TV presenter voice, or delivering a performance review of the host.

Everyone gets the same forfeits, so the stakes are the same whether you’re drinking or not. Nobody has to explain why they’re on lemonade, and the sober one never ends up as the referee.

Party games without drinking: FAQ

Is there a party game app for non-drinkers?

Yes. Antics is built on forfeits, not drinks, so non-drinkers play exactly the same game as everyone else. It’s free to download on iPhone and Android.

What party games can you play without alcohol?

All the good ones. Imposter, Would You Rather, Most Likely To, Never Have I Ever, Truth or Dare and The Four Kings all work sober, with a forfeit wherever a drink used to go. All six are in Antics.

Can drinkers and non-drinkers play together?

Yes, that’s the point. Everyone gets the same forfeits, and nobody has to sit out or explain themselves.

Is Antics a drinking game app?

No. Antics never tells anyone to drink. The penalties are forfeits, so it works the same at pre-drinks or at a sober night in.

Is Antics free?

It’s free to download. Imposter and The Four Kings are free to play, and every other game except Spicy comes with free cards. Antics Premium unlocks the full decks and Spicy.
