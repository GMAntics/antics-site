Pre-drinks games that aren’t drinking games

Pres is the hour where people are still arriving, someone’s still getting ready and nobody wants to learn the rules of anything. One phone passed round fixes it. Antics mode deals dares, votes and rules that stay live for a few cards, so the game rolls on as people come and go.

The app itself never tells anyone to drink, and nothing in it depends on drinking. It fits pres without being a drinking game, and the flatmate who isn’t drinking plays exactly the same game.

Freshers week: start gentle

In week one you’re playing with strangers, so open with games where nobody is put on the spot: Would You Rather, then Most Likely To. Never Have I Ever works once people relax, and Imposter gives the quiet ones a proper role.

Play it right: a dare or a forfeit should cost dignity, not wellbeing. Agree that anyone can swap one they won’t do, and look after your mates.

How it works

  1. Download Antics free on iPhone or Android. No account, no sign-up.
  2. Pick a game. Add names if you like, and some cards will call people out by name.
  3. Pass the phone round. Read the card out, do what it says, tap for the next one.
  4. Your phone remembers which cards it has shown, so nothing repeats until you’ve seen the lot.

Party games for uni: FAQ

What’s a good party game app for uni?

Antics puts nine games and thousands of hand-picked cards on one phone, from Never Have I Ever to Imposter. It’s free to start on iPhone and Android (it also runs on iPad), has no ads and no accounts, and works whether people are drinking or not.

What are good games for freshers week?

Start with Would You Rather and Most Likely To, because nobody is put on the spot. Play Never Have I Ever once people relax, and Imposter when everyone’s sitting down. All four are in Antics.

What are good pre-drinks games?

Ones that keep going while people arrive: Antics mode, What Are The Odds and The Four Kings. Antics never tells anyone to drink, so the whole flat can play.

Is Antics free?

It’s free to download. Imposter and The Four Kings are free to play, and every other game except Spicy comes with free cards. Antics Premium unlocks the full decks and Spicy.

Is Antics 18+?

Yes. It’s rated 18+ on the App Store and picked for adult groups. Spicy, the after dark deck, is part of Antics Premium.
