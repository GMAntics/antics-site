Quick answers

How do I contact Antics support?

Email support@anticsapp.com and a human (the developer) will reply, usually within a day or two.

How do I restore my Antics premium purchase?

Open Antics on a phone signed into the account you bought with (your Apple Account on iPhone, your Google account on Android), open the premium screen or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to that account, so they survive deleting the app or getting a new phone.

My referral code won't redeem. Why?

Each iPhone can redeem one referral code ever, and you can't redeem your own code. If none of those apply, email support@anticsapp.com with the code.

Is Antics free?

Free to download, with no ads and no sign-up. Imposter and The Four Kings are free to play, and every other game except Spicy comes with free cards. Antics Premium unlocks the full decks and Spicy: a weekly subscription (the first week is free for new subscribers on iPhone), a monthly subscription, or a one-off Forever purchase. Prices are shown in the app.

Contact

For any problem, question, refund query or idea, email support@anticsapp.com. Replies normally come within a day or two.

Restore your purchase

Premium is tied to the account you bought with, not your phone: your Apple Account on iPhone, your Google account on Android. If you've reinstalled the app or switched devices:

  • Make sure the phone is signed into the account you bought with
  • Open the premium screen or Settings in Antics and tap Restore Purchases
  • Still locked? Email us and we'll sort it

On iPhone, refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account. On Android, refunds are handled by Google Play: find the purchase in your Google Play order history and request one there.

Referral codes

Share your code and they get a free week of Premium on iPhone. A few rules that catch people out:

  • Each iPhone can redeem one referral code, ever
  • You can't redeem your own code

If a code still won't go through, email us with the code and we'll look it up.

Privacy

Antics has no accounts, no ads and no sign-up: the short version is that we never know who you are. The full detail lives in the privacy policy.

Found a bug?

Tell us what happened, what you expected, and your phone model and iOS or Android version. Screenshots help loads. Straight to support@anticsapp.com: bug reports get read first.
