How to play Never Have I Ever

Sit in a circle and everyone holds up five fingers. One player says "Never have I ever" and finishes the sentence with something they have genuinely never done. Everyone who has done it drops a finger, or takes a forfeit in the sober version, and the first player to lose all five fingers loses the round.
