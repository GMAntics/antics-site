Playing Never Have I Ever with the generator

  1. Everybody raises five fingers. Three makes for a quicker game.
  2. Whoever holds the phone taps Draw a card and reads the statement aloud. The cards are written in the first person, so it sounds like a confession even though nobody picked it.
  3. Anyone who has done the thing lowers one finger. The reader plays as well: with a generator choosing, nobody is safe.
  4. Whoever just lowered a finger owes the room the short version of the story. That is where the game really happens.
  5. Hand the phone left for the next card. The first person with no fingers left loses the round.

If your group likes a penalty, the loser can take one from our forfeit ideas, or you can simply keep a running tally. For the classic rules, and a longer list sorted by who is in the room, read the Never Have I Ever guide.

Never Have I Ever generator: short answers

Does the reader have to answer too?

Yes. In the classic game the speaker is safe, because they choose something they have never done. Here the generator chooses, so whoever reads is just as exposed as everyone else.

What if nobody puts a finger down?

Then the card was too tame for this room. Draw another, or move from Clean to Party if everyone is happy to go further.

Will we get repeats?

No. Each deck is shuffled once when the page opens and dealt through in full before anything comes round again. Swapping between Party and Clean starts a fresh shuffle of the deck you land on.

Can we play over a video call?

Yes. One person shares their screen with the generator open and reads each card while everybody holds their fingers up to the camera. Or tap Copy after a draw to drop the line into the call's chat.
