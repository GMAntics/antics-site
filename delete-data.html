Delete your data

Last updated: 6 September 2026

TL;DR: Antics (the party game app published by Antics App Ltd) has no accounts, so there is nothing to close. Deleting the app clears your phone. One email to us clears our servers.

What Antics holds about you

Nothing that names you. As the privacy policy explains, the app keeps a random anonymous ID on your phone and sends it with anonymous usage statistics (which games were opened, which cards were shown, plain launch counts) to our own servers. Purchases are keyed to an anonymous customer ID held by our purchase provider, RevenueCat. If you used the invite feature, a referral code is linked to that purchase ID. None of this is tied to your name, email, Apple ID or Google account.

Step 1: delete the app

Deleting Antics from your phone removes everything the app stored there: your gameplay progress, any player names you typed in, and the anonymous ID itself. On Android, a Google backup may bring the ID back if you reinstall; delete the app's backup data in your Google account settings if you want a completely clean slate.

Step 2: email us to clear our servers

Deleting the app does not delete the records already on our servers. To clear those, email support@anticsapp.com with the subject "Delete my Antics data" and, because nothing is keyed to your name, as much of this as you can:

  • whether you were on iPhone or Android
  • roughly when you installed the app
  • whether you bought anything in it, and roughly when
  • your referral code, if you ever shared one

We reply within 30 days and delete every record we can match to your install.

What gets deleted

  • the anonymous usage events recorded under your install's ID on our servers
  • the purchase records our purchase provider holds under your anonymous customer ID
  • any referral code and redemption records linked to that ID

What we cannot delete

  • Apple's and Google's own records of your purchases: those live in your App Store or Google Play account under their policies, not with us
  • aggregate statistics that no longer identify any install (for example, how many people played a game in a given month)

We keep no other data, and nothing we keep can identify you as a person.
