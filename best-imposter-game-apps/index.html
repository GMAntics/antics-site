ImposterEveryone sees the secret word except the imposter, who doesn’t get it. In Antics you can give them a one-word hint, and from six players you can switch on a second imposter.

Questions instead of cluesEach player answers a question about the word instead of giving a clue.

UndercoverThe undercover player gets a slightly different word, and Mr. White gets no word.

SpyEveryone but the spy sees a location, and you question each other. The spy wins by guessing the place.

Imposter game apps: FAQ

What’s the best Imposter game app?

We’d say Antics, but we make it, so we would. Imposter is free in Antics, with no ads and no account. It deals for 3 to 12 players on one phone, with word decks to mix, easy or hard hints and two-imposter rounds, and there are eight more games for when the room wants a change.

Is there a free Imposter game app?

Imposter is free in Antics: four word decks to play, and two more that unlock free when you follow Antics on Instagram or TikTok, with no ads. The other apps here are free to download, with more words or features as paid extras.

Can you play Imposter on one phone?

Yes. Antics deals the roles on one phone passed round, and each player holds to peek at theirs. You can also play free in your browser on our Imposter game page, with no download.

Is the Imposter game the same as Among Us?

No. Among Us is an online video game for 4 to 15 players, each on their own device. The Imposter word game is played face to face: everyone except the imposter sees a secret word, you take turns giving clues, then you vote.

What’s the difference between Imposter, Undercover and Spy?

In Imposter, the imposter doesn’t get the word, though some apps can give them a hint. In Undercover, the undercover player gets a slightly different word, and Mr. White gets none. In Spy, everyone except the spy sees a location and asks each other questions, while the spy tries to work out where you are.

How many players do you need for Imposter?

Three or more. Antics deals for 3 to 12 players, with a second imposter you can switch on from six players.
