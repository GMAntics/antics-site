This guide explains the traditional game as it is played offline. Antics for iOS is a party-only app built on forfeits and contains no drinking instructions.

How to play: ask someone "truth or dare?" They pick. Truth = answer honestly, dare = do the thing. Then it's their turn to ask someone. Refusing costs a forfeit, agreed before the game.

The two house rules that fix the game: no picking truth more than twice in a row (ends the coward problem), and the asker can't repeat a question someone else already used.

20 DARES No lame filler

A good dare has a cost the room can watch. Phones are the modern truth serum: half of these use one.

  1. Let the group scroll your camera roll for 20 seconds. You may veto ONE album.
  2. Show the group your screen-time report. No explanations allowed.
  3. Text your most recent match "you crossed my mind today x".
  4. Kiss someone in this room (cheek counts, cowards).
  5. Read out the last thing you typed in your notes app.
  6. Do your best impression of the player on your left until they laugh.
  7. Let the player on your right send one emoji from your phone to anyone they choose.
  8. Perform your go-to flirty dance move on the count of three.
  9. Swap phones with the player on your left until your next turn.
  10. Demonstrate your best chat-up line on the player on your left with full commitment.
  11. Moan the alphabet until someone laughs.
  12. Show the last five photos in your camera roll, no skips (one veto).
  13. Twerk for 15 seconds.
  14. Tell the group your honest first impression of everyone here, one by one.
  15. Sit on the lap of the player on your left until your next turn.
  16. Make up a 30-second rap about the person on your left.
  17. Impersonate another player until someone guesses who it is.
  18. Narrate everyone's movements like a nature documentary for the next two rounds.
  19. Sing everything you say for the next two rounds.
  20. Do your best catwalk across the room and back. Commit to it.

Refusals, penalties, and keeping it fun

Offline, Truth or Dare is often played with drinks. Set the refusal forfeit before the game instead (10 press-ups, speak in an accent for two rounds). Everyone gets one hard veto per game for anything they're genuinely not doing: the game's better when the pressure is theatrical, not real.

Truth or Dare FAQ

What are the rules of Truth or Dare?

One player asks another "truth or dare?" If they pick truth, they must answer a question honestly; if they pick dare, they must do the challenge. Then they become the asker. Refusing either costs an agreed forfeit.

What happens if someone refuses a dare?

They take the forfeit agreed before the game, and a second refusal earns a bigger one. Give everyone one no-questions-asked veto per game so nobody gets genuinely cornered.

Can you always pick truth?

House rule it: no more than two truths in a row is the standard fix. Without it, one coward can turn the game into an interview.

Is Truth or Dare OK to play without drinking?

Yes: it barely changes. A refusal costs a forfeit and everything else stays the same. It's the questions that carry the game.

Play it right: 18+ energy needs 18+ consent: the veto rule exists so dares stay funny instead of mean. Nothing here should end friendships, relationships, or employment. Look after your mates.
