What makes a good Truth or Dare app?

Dares you can do right nowNo props and no leaving the room. A dare should happen where everyone can watch.

Free cards you can actually playCheck what you get for free before any trial or subscription starts.

A way to say noAgree a pass or a forfeit before you start, so nobody feels cornered.

Truth or Dare apps: FAQ

What’s the best Truth or Dare app?

We’d say Antics, but we make it, so we would. Its truths and dares are about the people in the room, it has no ads and no sign-up, and nine more party games come in the same app.

Is there a free Truth or Dare app?

Antics is free to download, with free truths and dares to play and no ads. Antics Premium unlocks the full deck. You can also deal free truths and dares in your browser on our Truth or Dare generator, with no download.

Can you play Truth or Dare without an app?

Yes. Take turns asking someone “truth or dare?” They pick, then answer honestly or do the dare. Our Truth or Dare questions page has the rules and a long list to read from.

Can you play Truth or Dare as a drinking game?

Yes. Plenty of groups play it on a night out or at pre-drinks. The Antics app never tells anyone to drink and nothing in it depends on drinking, so the whole room plays the same game, drinking or not.

How many people do you need for Truth or Dare?

You can play with two, but it’s better with three or more, so there’s always someone watching the dare and turns still come round fast.

Does Antics have Christmas truths and dares?

Yes. Christmas truths and dares are free for everyone in Antics from 1 December to 1 January: tap Add Christmas on the game’s start screen. Our Christmas Truth or Dare page deals them free in your browser too.
