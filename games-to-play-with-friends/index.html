Games to play with friends

No board, no box, no "let me just read the rules". The best games for a group of friends need nothing but the people there and a phone, and they work whether you are on a sofa, in a group chat, or halfway through a night out.

The problem with most games is the admin: someone has to own the box, learn the rules, and keep the pieces. Pass-the-phone games skip all of it. One person reads a prompt, everyone answers, the next prompt comes. Here is what to play, sorted by the situation you are actually in.

On a night in

When you are all in one room, lead with the games that build. Never Have I Ever breaks the ice and gives the night its shared history; Most Likely To turns that history into an argument; Truth or Dare raises the stakes once everyone is warm.

Over the group chat

Not in the same room? The vote-and-answer games move straight to text. Post a Would You Rather and let everyone reply; drop a Never Have I Ever and watch who goes quiet. It is the easiest way to keep a night alive when you are scattered across the city.

→ Would You Rather: 32 dilemmas that split a room

When you are just bored

The fastest cure for a flat evening is What Are The Odds: dare a mate, name the odds, count down, and something happens. It needs zero setup and gets a dead room talking inside a minute. Most Likely To does the same job with less nerve required.

→ What Are The Odds: rules and 50 dares

Before a night out

While everyone is getting ready, you want games that scale as people arrive and do not stop the flow. All of the above work; if you want one game with a proper beginning and end, The Four Kings is the classic circle-of-cards game, forfeit-based so everyone plays the same game. Games to play with friends: FAQ

What games can you play with friends with no equipment?

Never Have I Ever, Truth or Dare, Most Likely To, Would You Rather and What Are The Odds all need nothing but the people there and, at most, one phone to read from. No board, no cards to buy, nothing to print.

What games can you play with friends over text?

Never Have I Ever, Would You Rather and Most Likely To all work in a group chat: one person posts the prompt and everyone replies. It is the easiest way to keep a night going when you are not in the same room.

What can you play when you are bored with friends?

Open one phone and deal a round of What Are The Odds or Most Likely To. They need zero setup and get a bored group talking within a minute. The Antics app runs all of these games from one phone.
