House rules

These are the rules the app shows before it deals a single card: everyone playing is 18+. Skip anything, no explanation needed. Keep it funny, not cruel.

What’s in the deck

Spicy mixes five kinds of card into one feed, so the room never settles:

  1. Never Have I Ever. Done it? Own up.
  2. Most Likely To. Point on three.
  3. Would You Rather. Vote on three.
  4. Truth or Dare. Chicken out? Your call.
  5. Rules and round-the-room challenges that keep everyone involved.

A taste

These are some of the tamer ones, picked for a public web page. The deck goes a good deal further.

MOST LIKELY TO Have a secret situationship right now.

MOST LIKELY TO Reply to “you up?” at 3am.

MOST LIKELY TO Fancy their housemate.

MOST LIKELY TO Deny sleeping with someone they have in fact slept with.

WOULD YOU RATHER Know who fancies you in this room, or never find out and always wonder?

WOULD YOU RATHER Two minutes and unforgettable, or an hour and fine?

WOULD YOU RATHER Your flat hears everything, once, or a stranger sees your search history, forever?

No forfeits here

Every other game in Antics hands out forfeits. Spicy doesn’t. Saying it out loud is the forfeit.

Who it’s for

House parties after midnight, couples’ nights, and any hen do or stag do where the group already knows too much about each other.

Questions people ask

Is Spicy free?

Spicy comes with Premium. On iPhone the first week is free, then it is a weekly or monthly subscription, or a one-off Forever purchase. Prices are shown in the app.

Can we skip a card?

Always. Tap past anything, no explanation needed.

Is it for couples or groups?

Both. It works with two people and it works with a full room.

What language is it in?

Spicy cards are in English for now, regardless of what language the rest of the app is in.

Ready when the room is

Spicy is in the Antics app for iPhone and Android. Strictly 18+.
